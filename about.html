About Shiftly / Team Operations

Keep Every Shift
In Sync.

A simple workspace for turning everyday shift notes into clearer handoffs, faster decisions, and a team that stays connected.

01 / CAPTURE

Start With The Shift

Crewmembers can quickly record what happened, what went well, and what needs attention from any phone or browser.

A consistent format keeps reports focused without adding another complicated task to the end of a shift.

02 / CONNECT

Keep Handoffs Clear

Shiftly aims to bridge the gap between management and crew by allowing easy submission of shift notes for manager review.

Everyone can see the information that matters to their role, while manager briefings remain private.

03 / INSIGHT

Find The Signal

AI-assisted briefings help managers identify wins, risks, and follow-up items without replacing the original employee notes.

The source report stays available so every briefing remains grounded in what the team actually shared.

04 / ALIGN

Move As A Team

A current Head's Up gives management one visible place to share priorities, reminders, and context with the whole crew.

Shiftly helps managers stay informed from anywhere while keeping the store aligned throughout the week.
