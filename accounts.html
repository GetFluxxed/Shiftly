04 / BUSINESS OWNER

Business access

These actions affect the business for your selected store. Business delegation and individual store access are managed separately.

Delegate business authority

An admin needs matching permissions in their store access. Shared catalog management can also be delegated separately to a store manager. Changing delegation ends their current sessions in this business.

Transfer your ownership

The selected person becomes an owner and your ownership is removed. You will be signed out.

Suspend or restore an account

Suspension ends access at every store. This is only available when you own every business associated with the account. Otherwise remove access to this store.
